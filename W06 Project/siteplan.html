Warsaw Guitars

Site Name: Warsaw Guitars

I chose "Warsaw Guitars" because it's a guitar webstore based in Warsaw.
The logo can be seen on the far-left side of the header.

Site Purpose:

The purpose is to market and buy used guitars. It will display information about the guitars, so the visitor understands what they're buying. Instead of a checkout, the visitor will be given a form to fill out and then will be contacted shortly by staff to discuss the guitar pick up. The form will also have an option to sell to the store your own guitar.

Senarios (visitor questions):

Are these guitars good quality?
Which guitar can I afford?

Colour Schema:

The colour schema is:
Fuchisa - #D8125B (main)
Dark Grey - #2C2E39 (secondary)
Old Lace - #FBF5E5 (accent 1)
Obsidian Black - #0d031b (accent 2)

Fuchisa is the main colour, and dark grey is the secondary, but will sometimes be used as an accent colour, such as the text-shadow in the header. The main and secondary colours will often be used as background colours for divs and sections. The two accent colours will mainly be used as text colours.

Typography:

The only font being used so far is the Raleway, sans-serif font.
